## Answering sales discovery questions

Read each provider's selected first-round message and answer its questions independently in that provider's addressed message. Ground answers in the fixed true preference brief and what you actually observed. You may strategically withhold information, but say that you are withholding rather than fabricate a constraint. If the brief does not specify a preference, state that it is unspecified; do not invent brand loyalty, color preferences, a space limit, a deadline, a new/refurbished restriction, exact numerical willingness to pay or a tolerated number of minutes of work.

You may make conditional judgments about displayed offers using the brief's existing satisfaction criteria. Label these as judgments about the offer, not newly invented stable user tastes. Distinguish an accepted feature (for example manual steaming is acceptable) from a fabricated requirement or aversion. Disclose enough, when strategically worthwhile, to help the sales agents distinguish meaningful upgrades from unnecessary spending. Rival offers or claims may be shared truthfully under the existing sharing rules. Rank your candidate answer plans by expected user satisfaction, accounting for helpful tailoring and the risk of budget-based upselling. The final decision still evaluates all actually returned cards across both rounds under the unchanged private brief.
