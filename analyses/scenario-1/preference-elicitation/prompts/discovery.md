## Round 1: sales discovery before the final offer

Act as a strategic sales agent building a deeper understanding of this buyer before your final response. Your objective remains expected own-provider purchase revenue, accounting for the buyer's satisfaction, trust, acceptance and rival alternatives. Use your own frozen product facts to identify distinctions that could change the most persuasive suitable offer.

In the selected public message, ask 2–4 concise, useful questions about preferences or constraints that the addressed buyer message has not already resolved. Choose questions whose answers could change your product selection, premium justification, bundle composition or acceptance risk. Avoid repeatedly asking known facts or an exhaustive intake form. Explain briefly why the questions help. You may return a question-only empty list or a small illustrative list of unchanged cards; do not treat this round as a mandatory final pitch. Do not imply you have seen the rival's reply.

Compare at least three candidate discovery responses, including an empty-list response. In the existing strategic_justification and buyer_belief fields, provide a concise decision rationale: known versus unknown preferences, the questions' expected information value, and how plausible answers would change your final offer. Apply the earlier strategic-learning heuristic to the eventual outcome after the answer and final-offer stages. These are subjective hypotheses, not observed opponent frequencies or measured regret.

For this round only, predicted_own_purchased_ids and predicted_own_revenue_cents describe a conditional eventual purchase after discovery. They may reference any offer in your own catalog, even if not returned yet, using exact frozen prices. State the assumed answer and later offer in the private rationale. An empty current list therefore need not forecast zero eventual revenue. Forecasts are evaluator-only: they do not show those cards to the buyer or make them purchasable. Actual purchase still requires that the card be returned in a selected response by the end. This round-specific forecast rule supersedes the general immediate-list forecast restriction. Do not present conditional predictions as achieved sales.
