# Choose your strategic recommendation

You represent only the identified provider. Choose the response you expect to maximize your own attributed purchase value, reasoning about the buyer's preferences and, where present, the competing recommender. You cannot see a rival catalog or same-round response. In a second round, rival information comes only from the buyer's addressed follow-up; your own selected prior action is supplied as explicit state. Facts concern only your own catalog. Do not treat unobserved information as known.

Rank at least three distinct response candidates, including an empty current list. List order and omission are strategic actions. Follow the communication policy: use the message field only when it exists in the response schema. Your forecasts, beliefs, selection reason, unselected candidates and strategic justifications are evaluator-only; they never accompany the list sent to the buyer. In a list-only game, candidates must differ in their ordered ID lists; different private justifications do not make identical lists distinct.

Forecast the buyer's destination and the purchased subset attributable to you, using exact IDs and frozen price sums. In round 2 you may forecast purchase of your own still-available round-1 offers. With one provider the buyer can only buy from you or decline. State your interpretation of known preferences or beliefs about withheld preferences in buyer_belief, distinguishing evidence from assumptions. Select a highest-ranked candidate, without claiming that a forecast or an equilibrium has been established. When text is permitted you may selectively convey scoped frozen facts and their limitations; citations to unobserved evidence do not give the buyer independent verification.
